Texas Community Property Laws and Selling Your Houston Home During Divorce

Published August 26, 2026 · Houston, Texas
Back to All Articles

Going through a divorce in Houston is emotionally challenging, and understanding Texas community property laws adds another layer of complexity when selling your home. As one of only nine community property states, Texas has specific rules that affect how marital assets, including real estate, are divided during divorce proceedings.

Understanding Texas Community Property Laws

Texas follows community property laws, meaning that most assets acquired during marriage are owned equally by both spouses, regardless of whose name appears on the title. Your Houston home is typically considered community property if purchased during the marriage, even if only one spouse is on the mortgage or deed. This equal ownership extends to the home equity and any appreciation in value that occurred during the marriage.

There are exceptions to community property rules. Property owned before marriage, gifts received by one spouse, or inheritances generally remain separate property. However, if separate property becomes commingled with marital funds or if marital funds were used for improvements, the situation becomes more complex and may require legal clarification.

Selling Your Houston Home During Divorce

When divorcing couples in Houston decide to sell their home, both spouses typically must agree to the sale since both own the property equally. This can create challenges if one spouse wants to sell while the other prefers to keep the home. The proceeds from the sale are usually split 50-50, though couples can negotiate different arrangements as part of their divorce settlement.

Timing matters significantly when selling a marital home. Some couples choose to sell before finalizing the divorce to simplify asset division, while others wait until after the decree. Selling during divorce proceedings can help both parties move forward financially and emotionally, avoiding the complications of shared ownership after separation.

Traditional home sales in Houston can take 60-90 days or longer, requiring both spouses to cooperate on showings, negotiations, and closing details. This extended timeline can increase stress and conflict during an already difficult period. Market preparation, repairs, and staging also require joint decisions and financial investment that divorcing couples may struggle to coordinate.

The Cash Sale Alternative for Divorcing Houston Homeowners

Many divorcing couples in the Houston area choose to sell their home for cash to simplify the process and accelerate their fresh start. Cash sales eliminate financing contingencies, reduce the timeline to just days instead of months, and require no repairs or improvements. Both spouses can move forward without the burden of maintaining a shared property or coordinating traditional sale activities.

A cash offer provides certainty in an uncertain time. You will know exactly how much money to expect and when you will receive it, making it easier to plan your post-divorce finances. There are no surprises from inspection negotiations or deals falling through due to buyer financing issues.
