Understanding the Foreclosure Timeline in Houston

In Texas, foreclosure can happen quickly. Once you miss payments, your lender can begin the foreclosure process in as little as 120 days. Texas is a non-judicial foreclosure state, which means lenders do not need to go through court to foreclose on your property. After receiving a Notice of Default and a Notice of Sale, your home can be auctioned on the courthouse steps, typically on the first Tuesday of the month. This tight timeline makes it critical to act fast if you want to sell your home before foreclosure in Houston.

Why Selling Before Foreclosure Is Better Than Letting It Happen

Allowing a foreclosure to complete can have devastating long-term consequences. A foreclosure stays on your credit report for seven years and can drop your credit score by 200 points or more. This makes it extremely difficult to buy another home, rent an apartment, or even get approved for credit cards. You may also be responsible for any deficiency balance if your home sells at auction for less than what you owe. Additionally, foreclosure involves legal fees, court costs, and the emotional stress of losing your home involuntarily. By selling your Houston home before foreclosure, you take control of the situation, potentially walk away with cash in hand, and protect your financial future.
