Legal Considerations When Selling During Divorce

Before selling your Houston home during divorce, understand the legal requirements. In Texas, both spouses typically must agree to sell marital property. Consult with your divorce attorney to ensure the sale aligns with your settlement agreement. Determine how proceeds will be divided before listing or accepting offers. If there's a mortgage, understand the payoff amount and how remaining equity will be split. Some couples include the home sale terms directly in their divorce decree, while others handle it separately. Working with an experienced cash buyer who understands divorce situations can simplify the legal logistics and timeline.
