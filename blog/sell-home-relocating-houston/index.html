Understanding Your Timeline Challenges

When relocating for work, you are juggling multiple deadlines at once. Your new employer expects you to start on a specific date, your family needs time to adjust, and your Houston home needs to sell. The traditional listing process typically takes 60-90 days in the Houston area, which may be longer than you have available.

Beyond the time crunch, you are also dealing with coordinating showings from another city, managing repairs while packing, and the stress of potentially carrying two mortgages. These challenges make relocating for work one of the most stressful home selling situations.
