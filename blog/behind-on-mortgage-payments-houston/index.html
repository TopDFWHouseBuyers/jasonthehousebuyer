Behind on Mortgage Payments in Houston? Here Are Your Options

Published September 09, 2026 · Houston, Texas
Back to All Articles

If you are behind on mortgage payments in Houston, you are not alone. Many homeowners face financial hardship due to job loss, medical bills, divorce, or unexpected expenses. The good news is that you have several options to explore before foreclosure becomes inevitable.

Understanding Your Situation When Behind on Mortgage Payments

When you fall behind on your mortgage, time is critical. Most lenders begin foreclosure proceedings after 90-120 days of missed payments. In Texas, foreclosure can happen quickly - sometimes in as little as 41 days after the initial notice. This makes it essential to act fast and understand all your available options.

Houston homeowners facing this challenge should know that Texas is a non-judicial foreclosure state, meaning lenders can foreclose without going to court. This speeds up the process significantly compared to other states, leaving you with less time to respond.

Option 1: Contact Your Lender About Loan Modification

Your first step should be contacting your lender immediately. Many Houston lenders offer loss mitigation programs designed to help homeowners avoid foreclosure. These may include:

  • Loan modification to reduce monthly payments
  • Forbearance agreements that temporarily pause payments
  • Repayment plans that spread missed payments over time
  • Refinancing to secure better terms

Lenders prefer these solutions over foreclosure because foreclosure is expensive for them too. Be prepared to provide financial documentation showing your hardship and ability to make modified payments.

Option 2: Consider a Short Sale

If you owe more on your mortgage than your Houston home is worth, a short sale might be an option. This involves selling your home for less than the outstanding mortgage balance with your lender's approval. While this impacts your credit, it is typically less damaging than foreclosure and allows you to walk away without owing the difference.

Short sales in Houston can take 3-6 months to complete, requiring patience and extensive paperwork. You will need lender approval, and the process can be complicated with multiple parties involved.

Option 4: Declare Bankruptcy

Filing for bankruptcy can temporarily stop foreclosure through an automatic stay. Chapter 13 bankruptcy allows you to catch up on missed payments through a repayment plan over 3-5 years. However, bankruptcy has serious long-term credit consequences and should be considered carefully with an attorney.

What Happens If You Do Nothing?

Ignoring the problem will not make it go away. If you do nothing when behind on mortgage payments in Houston, here is what typically happens:

  • Your lender files a Notice of Default
  • Your home is scheduled for foreclosure auction
  • Your credit score drops significantly (100-300 points)
  • You may owe a deficiency balance after sale
  • Foreclosure stays on your credit report for 7 years

The Texas foreclosure process moves quickly, so taking action now is essential to protect your financial future.
