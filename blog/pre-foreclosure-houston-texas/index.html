What Is Pre-Foreclosure in Houston Texas?

Pre-foreclosure is the period between when your mortgage lender files a Notice of Default and when your home is actually auctioned at a foreclosure sale. In Texas, this process typically begins after you have missed three to four mortgage payments. Your lender will send you a demand letter requiring you to bring your loan current, and if you cannot do so, they will accelerate the loan and demand full payment.

Texas operates under a non-judicial foreclosure process, which means foreclosures can happen relatively quickly compared to other states. From the time you receive your first notice of default, you may have as little as 21 days before your home is sold at auction on the first Tuesday of the month at the county courthouse.

Understanding Your Rights During Pre-Foreclosure

Even though you have fallen behind on payments, you still have important rights as a homeowner in Houston. Texas law requires your lender to provide you with at least 20 days notice before the foreclosure sale. You also have the right to reinstate your loan by paying all past-due amounts plus fees and costs up until the day before the foreclosure sale.

Additionally, you have the right to sell your home during the pre-foreclosure period. Many Houston homeowners do not realize that selling their property before the foreclosure auction can help them avoid the severe credit damage of a completed foreclosure and potentially walk away with cash in hand if there is equity in the home.

Options When Facing Pre-Foreclosure in Houston

You have several options to consider when dealing with pre-foreclosure in the Greater Houston area including Katy, Sugar Land, The Woodlands, and surrounding communities. First, you can attempt a loan modification with your lender to restructure your payments. Second, you can pursue a short sale where the lender agrees to accept less than what is owed. Third, you can file for bankruptcy to temporarily halt the foreclosure process.

However, one of the fastest and most straightforward options is selling your home to a cash buyer. This eliminates the uncertainty of listing with a realtor, waiting for buyer financing, and hoping to close before the foreclosure date. A cash sale can close in as little as seven days, giving you a clean exit from a difficult situation.
