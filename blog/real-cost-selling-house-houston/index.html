The Real Cost of Selling a House in Houston - Fees and Hidden Costs

Published October 02, 2026 · Houston, Texas
Back to All Articles

Selling your Houston home involves more expenses than most homeowners anticipate. Understanding these costs upfront helps you make informed decisions and avoid unpleasant surprises at closing.

Traditional Real Estate Commission Fees

The largest expense when selling a house in Houston is typically the real estate agent commission. In the Greater Houston area, you can expect to pay 5-6% of your home's sale price in commission fees, split between your listing agent and the buyer's agent. On a $300,000 home, that amounts to $15,000-$18,000 right off the top. This percentage is negotiable, but reducing it may limit your agent's motivation or the pool of buyer's agents willing to show your property.

Closing Costs and Title Fees

Beyond commissions, Houston home sellers face several closing costs. Title insurance, required in Texas real estate transactions, typically costs $1,000-$3,000 depending on your home's value. You may also pay for the title search, recording fees, and transfer taxes. Attorney fees, if you hire a real estate lawyer, add another $500-$1,500. In total, expect closing costs to consume 1-3% of your sale price. Many sellers in Katy, Sugar Land, and The Woodlands are surprised when these fees total $5,000-$10,000 or more.

Pre-Sale Repairs and Improvements

Most Houston homes need some preparation before listing. Buyers in competitive markets like Cypress, Tomball, and Pearland expect move-in ready properties. Fresh paint typically costs $2,000-$5,000 for an entire home. Carpet replacement runs $1,500-$4,000. HVAC repairs or replacement can exceed $5,000-$10,000, critical in Houston's climate. Roof repairs, foundation work, or plumbing issues often total $3,000-$15,000 or more. These repairs become non-negotiable when inspection reports reveal problems to potential buyers.

Home Staging and Marketing Expenses

Professional staging helps homes in The Woodlands, League City, and Friendswood sell faster, but costs $2,000-$5,000 monthly. Professional photography runs $300-$600, while virtual tours add another $150-$400. Some agents include these services, but others charge separately or expect you to cover costs upfront.

Carrying Costs While Listed

The average home in Houston stays on the market 30-60 days, sometimes longer. During this period, you continue paying your mortgage, property taxes, insurance, utilities, and HOA fees. In areas like Missouri City, Humble, and Conroe, these carrying costs can total $2,000-$4,000 monthly. If your home takes three months to sell, that's an additional $6,000-$12,000 expense many sellers overlook when calculating their net proceeds.

Hidden Costs That Surprise Houston Sellers

Several unexpected expenses often emerge during the selling process. Homeowner association documents and transfer fees cost $200-$500. Survey certificates required by some buyers add $350-$600. Termite inspections and treatment can cost $300-$2,000. If negotiating after inspection, buyer-requested repairs or credits often total $2,000-$8,000. Some buyers in Baytown and Pasadena request the seller pay their closing costs, which can reduce your net proceeds by another 2-3%.

Calculating Your True Net Proceeds

When adding all costs together, the total expense of selling a house in Houston typically ranges from 8-15% of your sale price. On a $300,000 home, you might pay $24,000-$45,000 in various fees, meaning you'd net $255,000-$276,000 before paying off your existing mortgage. This reality shocks many homeowners who assumed they'd pocket the full sale price.
